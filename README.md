Student thesis about Stock prediction.
One Model for choosing 10 Stocks out of a universe using SDE/Monte-Carlo Simulation.
Three next-day price prediction models: Linear, SVR, TimesFM-zero-shot.
Evaluation: 50-52% DA, Best Model (SVR) returned +6.6% on average with 0.20 sharpe, beating buy-and-hold on 6 out of 10 stocks but not on average.
